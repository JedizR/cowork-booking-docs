# Glossary

Terms of Cowork Booking, one table per context. Each row follows the template term · context · meaning · counterexample · status/source. The status starts with Decided, Stakeholder-clarified, Observed or Superseded, then the source in brackets: the D number first where one applies, then the row of "Disputes decided in M1" (DECISIONS.md) that reads it more precisely (row 7 for the derived Completed state). "ADR (M2): <topic>" names an ADR that M2 must write; RULES.md lists the topics.

The course calls the three contexts "Purchase", "Payments" and "Integrations/Locks" ([syllabus site]; the [extraction site] also says "Access"). This project names them Purchase, Payment and Access (PUR-T33, PMT-T01, AXS-T01).

The same word can mean different things in different contexts, and each row says which context it belongs to. For example: "Operator" is a Member with is_operator in Purchase (PUR-T02), Payment's operator page uses its own password (PMT-T18), and the kiosk is run by Staff in Access (AXS-T14). "Held" is a booking status in Purchase (PUR-T20), not the class term "held slot". "Paid" is a Payment status (PMT-T04), never a coverage (PUR-T24). "Expired" is a booking status in Purchase (hold lapsed unpaid, PUR-T19), a session status in Payment (PMT-T03) and a derived grant condition in Access (AXS-T05). "Completed" (Purchase, derived, PUR-T22) is not the Payment session status "complete". A revoked grant shows the badge "Cancelled" (AXS-R10), but its state is revoked (AXS-T04).

Example data used across the three contexts: clock 2026-10-05 10:00 Bangkok (Monday). Spaces: Meeting Room A (space_id 1, capacity 6, THB 300 per hour), Focus Pod 1 (space_id 2, capacity 2, THB 20 per hour), Board Room (space_id 3, capacity 12, THB 1,000 per hour), Community Table (space_id 4, capacity 8, THB 0 per hour). Standard booking BK-7KQ2M9: Member A, Meeting Room A, 2026-10-07 09:00-10:30 (3 blocks), party size 4, THB 450.00 (amount_satang 45000), hold_expires_at 2026-10-05 10:15, payment session expires_at 10:13, ticket code H7K3-9QXA. Member A pays by card (plan_active false); Member B has plan_active true.

## Purchase

| PUR-T07 | Archived space | Purchase | A space with `archived_at` set. Hidden from browsing and booking. Its bookings and history stay. | Not deleted. Not a space that is only closed for a day. | Decided (D23) |
| PUR-T08 | Bangkok time | Purchase | The fixed UTC+7 offset used for dates, opening hours, pages and JSON output. Instants are stored as timestamptz. In JSON a time without an offset is an error, not Bangkok time. | Decided (D2) |
| PUR-T09 | Block | Purchase | A 30-minute unit that starts at :00 or :30 Bangkok time. The unit of booking and of price. Example: 09:00-09:30. | 09:15 is not a block start. | Decided (D4) |
| PUR-T10 | Duration | Purchase | The number of blocks booked: 1 to 8 (30 min to 4 h). The end is start + blocks x 30 min and is exclusive. Example: 3 blocks, 09:00-10:30. | 9 blocks, or 45 minutes, is not a duration. | Decided (D4) |
| PUR-T11 | Opening hours | Purchase | 08:00-20:00 Bangkok time, every day. A booking starts at or after 08:00 and ends at or before 20:00 on the same date. The last 1-block start is 19:30. | 19:30 for 2 blocks runs to 20:30 and is outside opening hours. | Decided (D3) |
| PUR-T12 | Notice period | Purchase | A booking must start at least 60 min after `clock.now()`. Example: at 2026-10-05 10:00 the first start is 11:00. | 10:30 the same day is too soon. | Decided (D5) |
| PUR-T13 | Booking horizon | Purchase | The last start date a Member may book: today + 30 days (Bangkok date). Example: on 2026-10-05 the last date is 2026-11-04. | 2026-11-05 is beyond the horizon. | Decided (D5) |
| PUR-T14 | Buffer | Purchase | The gap kept free between two bookings of one space: 0 min. Back-to-back bookings are allowed. | A cleaning gap is an open question (PUR-Q01), not a rule. | Decided (D6) |
| PUR-T15 | Start-block grid | Purchase | For one space, one date and one duration: every start block from 08:00 to 19:30, each marked available or greyed with a reason ("Too soon", "Runs past 20:00", "Booked"). | Not a "free right now" flag. Not a promise: a race can still take an available start. | Decided (D4, D11) |
| PUR-T16 | Party size | Purchase | The number of people who will use the room: a whole number from 1 to the space's capacity, asked on the booking form. Example: 4 in Meeting Room A. | Not seats sold: a party of 1 still takes the whole room. | Decided (D7) |
| PUR-T17 | Booking | Purchase | Purchase's reservation record: reference, Member, space, start, end, blocks, party size, booking price, coverage, status, `hold_expires_at`, `payment_session_id`, payment outcome, cancel reason, refund amount, reason and outcome, grant status (the follow-up fields, PUR-T34). | Not a payment session (Payment) and not an access grant (Access). | PUR-T18 | Booking reference | Purchase | `BK-` + 6 symbols from 23456789ABCDEFGHJKMNPQRSTVWXYZ, unique. The shared reference that Payment and Access store. Example: BK-7KQ2M9. | Not an entry credential: check-in never accepts it. Not the internal row id. | Decided (D22) |
| PUR-T19 | Booking status | Purchase | The stored state of a booking: held, confirmed, expired or cancelled. It moves held to confirmed, expired or cancelled, and confirmed to cancelled. | "Paid", "Completed" and "No-show" are not stored statuses. | Decided (D11, D18) |
| PUR-T20 | Held booking | Purchase | A pay booking waiting for payment, status held, with `hold_expires_at` = creation + 15 min. Example: BK-7KQ2M9 created 10:00, hold until 10:15. | Not the class "held slot" (any booking, paid or not). A held booking whose hold has lapsed no longer blocks the slot. | Decided (D11) |
| PUR-T21 | Slot-blocking booking | Purchase | A booking with status confirmed, or status held with `hold_expires_at` later than `clock.now()`. Every availability read and the booking insert use this predicate. | An expired or cancelled booking, or a held booking at or after its `hold_expires_at`, does not block. | Decided (D11) |
| PUR-T22 | Completed booking | Purchase | A confirmed booking with `clock.now()` at or after its end (from 2026-10-07 10:30 for BK-7KQ2M9). Purchase derives it from the status and `clock.now()` when it shows the booking; it is never stored. JSON shows "completed": true beside "status": "confirmed". | "No-show" is derived by Access from its scan log, never by Purchase. Not the Payment session status "complete". | Decided (D11; Disputes decided in M1, row 7) |
| PUR-T23 | Booking price (agreed price) | Purchase | The agreed price of one booking: `agreed_price_satang` = round_half_up(hourly rate in satang x blocks / 2), set at creation, stored, never recalculated. Example: THB 450.00, amount_satang 45000. | Not the hourly rate. Not proof of payment. Payment collects it and never re-prices it. | Stakeholder-clarified (course instructor, class rules PR #14 at 25ca71e) for bookings not covered by a subscription; the stored price on plan bookings is Decided (D8, D10) |
| PUR-T24 | Coverage | Purchase | How a booking is settled, fixed at creation: pay (card through Payment), plan (the Member's `plan_active` was true), free (price 0). | “Covered” is not always “money collected” [extraction site]: pay coverage collects money through Payment; plan and free coverage collect none, so a plan booking is covered but never paid. | Decided (D10) |
| PUR-T25 | Plan | Purchase | The Member flag `plan_active`, set only by the Operator. While it is true, new bookings get coverage plan. Example: Member B. | Not a subscription keyed by a typed name. Not sold in v1 (PUR-Q02). | Decided (D10) |
| PUR-T26 | Reconciliation | Purchase | Reading a held booking's payment session (GET /payment-sessions/{id}) and applying the result: paid goes to fulfilment, unpaid after the hold becomes expired, unpaid inside the hold changes nothing. | Not a webhook and not a timer: it runs only when Purchase touches the booking. | Decided (D13) |
| PUR-T27 | Fulfilment | Purchase | Confirming a held booking from a paid session whose booking reference, amount and currency equal the booking's, then requesting its grant. | A paid session with a different amount is not fulfilled: the booking is cancelled (amount_mismatch) and refunded in full. | Decided (D14) |
| PUR-T28 | Cancellation | Purchase | Setting a booking to cancelled with a reason, a refund amount and the outcomes of the follow-up calls. The row stays. | Not a delete. | Decided (D18, D19) |
| PUR-T29 | Cancellation refund | Purchase | The refund the policy gives, shown before the person confirms: 100% or 0% of the booking price for pay coverage, always 0 for plan or free. Stored with its outcome and attempt number. The reason Purchase sends to Payment is member_cancel or operator_cancel; the full refunds of D14 use amount_mismatch or slot_unavailable. | No partial refund other than 0% or 100%. Revoking access alone implies no refund. | Decided (D18) |
| PUR-T30 | Pending follow-up | Purchase | A cross-service step that got no answer (timeout or connection error), so no result is stored, and waits for an idempotent retry: e-ticket "being prepared", "revocation pending" or "refund pending". A refund also stays pending while it waits for its revoke (PUR-Q10). | A refund that Payment returned as failed is not pending: only the Operator starts attempt+1. | Decided (D19, D20) |
| PUR-T31 | Counted member | Purchase | A Member account with at least one confirmed booking in the metrics period (the last 7 Bangkok days). | Not a distinct typed name. One account with two bookings counts once. | Decided (D24) |
| PUR-T32 | Utilization | Purchase | Confirmed booked hours in the metrics period divided by (12 h x non-archived spaces x 7). Example: 3.5 h over 4 spaces is 3.5 / 336 = 1.0%. | Plan and free hours count; held, expired and cancelled hours do not. Not revenue. | Decided (D24) |
| PUR-T33 | Purchase (context) | Purchase | The context and service that owns members, spaces, 30-minute availability, bookings, price, coverage, booking status and cancellation. It is the only caller of the other two services. The course uses the same name: Purchase "Owns price and coverage" [extraction site]. Service `cowork-booking-purchase`, port 8001. | Purchase collects no money (Payment) and issues no access (Access). It never reads another service's database. | Decided (D13, D15; ADR (M2): three contexts; course [extraction site]) |
| PUR-T34 | Follow-up fields | Purchase | Purchase's own record, on the booking, of what the other services answered. grant_status: not_requested, pending (asked, no answer yet; shown "being prepared"), issued, revoke_pending or revoked. payment_outcome: not_required (plan or free), unpaid or paid, the last payment_status read; a read with no answer stores nothing and the page says "Payment status unknown, refresh later". refund_status: none, pending, succeeded or failed, with refund_attempt (1, 2, ...). cancel_reason: member_cancel, operator_cancel or amount_mismatch. refund_reason: member_cancel, operator_cancel, amount_mismatch or slot_unavailable. JSON links: payment_url (the hosted page, held pay bookings only) and ticket_url (once issued). Example: BK-7KQ2M9 after Member A cancels: grant_status revoked, refund_status succeeded, refund_attempt 1, cancel_reason member_cancel. | Not the Access grant state: grant_status is never checked_in and never a no-show, which only Access knows. Not the Payment session status. "Reason" alone is ambiguous: say cancel_reason or refund_reason. | Decided (D13, D19, D20) |

## Payment

| ID | Term | Context | Meaning | Counterexample | Status / source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PMT-T01 | Payment (context) | Payment | The context and service that collects an agreed amount on a hosted page, records payment attempts and refunds, and reports outcomes when Purchase asks. Payment answers calls; it never calls another service. The course calls it "Payments" ("Owns payment outcomes"). Service `cowork-booking-payment`, port 8002. | Payment sets no price, decides no coverage, changes no booking status and issues no access. | PMT-T10 | Test card | Payment | A card number with a fixed mock outcome, listed on the test-mode banner: 4242424242424242 success; 4000000000000002 generic_decline; 4000000000009995 insufficient_funds; 4000000000000069 expired_card; 4000000000000119 processing_error; 4000000000005126 success, then its first refund fails. | PMT-T17 | Payment API token | Payment | PAYMENT_API_TOKEN: the bearer token Purchase sends on every Payment API call. ## Access

| ID | Term | Context | Meaning | Counterexample | Status / source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AXS-T01 | Access | Access | The context and service that owns access grants, their issuance, the ticket code, the e-ticket and the check-in kiosk.
