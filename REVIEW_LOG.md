# Review log

The M3 docs review gate runs in rounds. Each round runs six independent lens reviewers in parallel: business owner, member UX, operator/staff, cross-document consistency, security/privacy and testability. Each finding is labelled blocker (the docs contradict each other or a required journey cannot be built or tested), major (a builder or tester would get it wrong, or a real user or business gap) or minor (wording, a missing example, a cheap clarification). Duplicate findings are merged, and each merged finding is either applied, rejected with a reason, or left open. The gate passes when a round brings no new blocker or major finding. After round 5 the remaining non-blockers move to OPEN_QUESTIONS.md, and the gate passes if no blocker remains. The M4 contract sign-offs are logged here too.
